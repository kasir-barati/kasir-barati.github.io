React Component Organization

⚛️

1. Flat

Small apps 

└── components/
    ├── Button.jsx
    ├── Header.jsx
    └── Modal.jsx

2. Feature-Based

Makes business domains the organizing principle 

└── features/
    ├── auth/
    │   ├── components/
    │   ├── hooks/
    │   └── api/
    └── cart/
└── components/  (shared)
└── hooks/
└── pages/
└── utils/
3. Atomic Design

Makes UI composition the organizing principle of the whole app 

└── atoms/
└── molecules/
└── organisms/
└── templates/
└── pages/

✅ Key Principles

  • •Colocation: component, styles, hooks, tests together.
  • •Single Responsibility: one component per file.
  • •Feature isolation: group by domain.
  • •Shared vs local: components/ vs features/x/.
  • •Naming: PascalCase.jsx, useX.js.

💡 Why It Matters

Reusability
Obvious shared components
Scalability
Add/remove features safely
Discoverability
Predictable file locations
Testability
Tests beside code (Storybook, and Jest)
Maintainability
Changes stay local
Team scaling
Fewer merge conflicts

🎯 Last Word

The best structure is one your team understands & that matches your app's domain.
